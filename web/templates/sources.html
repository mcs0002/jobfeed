How sources work Every automated source passes through four stages: source scope (the full configured board or a server-side facet/search subset) → pre-filter (irreversible title and location rules remove senior roles, excluded locations, pre-degree roles, and obvious operational, technology, HR, retail, or other non-finance noise) → tag (an LLM labels area, desk, seniority, job type, location, work mode, language, and other requirements) → Browse filters (your reversible display preferences). Toggling a firm or category off here greys it and drops its roles from Browse and Stats.
Full board every role on the configured board/feed · Positive facet structured source-side subset · Search scope server-side keyword query · Manual no automated source; usually email, LinkedIn, or prose-only · heavy = isolated execution for big/slow boards. Markers: ↗ career site · ⓘ note (hover) · 🎓 separate graduate scheme on the firm's own site.
